# Research note: FPT (2022–2025)

## Executive summary

- FPT's revenue grew from VND 44,010 bn in 2022 to VND 70,113 bn in 2025, representing a CAGR of 16.8%.
- Net income also increased from VND 6,491 bn in 2022 to VND 11,232 bn in 2025, with a CAGR of 20.1%.
- The company maintained double-digit growth in 2025 despite global economic uncertainties. [1][2]

## Revenue

- In 2025, FPT's revenue reached VND 70,113 bn, which is a growth of 11.6% compared to 2024.
- The Technology sector continues to be the main growth driver, contributing 63,4% of total revenue, equivalent to 44.475 billion VND, in 2025. [3][4]
- Specifically, IT services for foreign markets grew by 14,3% in 2025, with newly signed revenue exceeding 1,5 billion USD. [1]
- In 2024, domestic IT services and the Made by FPT ecosystem also recorded growth of 13,9% and 31,0% respectively. [5]

## Profitability

- FPT's gross profit margin was 36.9% in 2025, placing it in the percentile 89 of its sector.
- Net profit margin was 16.0% in 2025 compared to 14.7% in 2022, reflecting better operational efficiency and cost control. [1][6]
- ROE was 28.3% in 2025, which is in the percentile 100 of the sector.

## Cash flow and capital structure

- Cash flow from operations (CFO) was VND 10,136 bn in 2025, down from VND 11,704 bn in 2024.
- Free cash flow (FCF) was VND 5,038 bn in 2025, representing an FCF margin of 7.2%.
- FPT paid dividends of VND 4,574 bn in 2025, up from VND 3,292 bn in 2024.

## Risk signals

- Borrowings rose 41.0% during the year; debt stands at 0.48x of equity.
- FPT faces financial risks such as exchange rate fluctuations in key markets, bad debts, and high inventory. [7]
- Additionally, strategic risks include potential misdirection in technology investments and intense competition from foreign and domestic players. [8]
- The company's receivables growth exceeded revenue growth by +9.3 pp in 2025, with days sales outstanding (DSO) increasing by 5 days.

## Forecast

- For 2026, the model forecasts a baseline revenue of VND 55,549 bn (representing a growth of 9.8%), with a lower bound of VND 44,123 bn (at a growth rate of -12.8%) and an upper bound of VND 69,932 bn at an 80.0% coverage.
- This forecast is driven by total asset growth contributing +2.0 pp and inventory growth contributing +1.8 pp, offset by asset turnover contributing -0.7 pp.
- In comparison, FPT's own plan for 2026 targets a revenue of 58.580 billion VND, representing a 15,8% growth compared to the adjusted 2025 baseline of VND 50,606 bn. [9]
- The company's 2026 strategy focuses on investing in and commercializing strategic technologies like AI, quantum technology, and cybersecurity. [10]

## Limits and caveats

- Peer comparison uses the ICB sector Công nghệ Thông tin (18 companies, 2025)
- From 1 January 2026 FPT no longer consolidates FPT Telecom and accounts for it under the equity method, so consolidated revenue no longer includes FPT Telecom's revenue (source: https://vietstock.vn/2026/03/fpt-dieu-chinh-cach-hach-toan-ket-qua-kinh-doanh-fpt-telecom-tu-nam-2026-737-1413810.htm)
- The 2025 revenue base excludes FOX's revenue. This is an estimate: intra-group sales are not added back, and the growth rate still comes from figures that include FOX.
- The forecast assumes the market grows at its historical median; revenue volatility group of FPT: ≤ 5%.

## Confidence

- **Figures:** high. Every number is filled in from the database, none is written by the language model.
- **Qualitative statements:** 9 of 9 are fully supported by their passages, 0 partly.
- **Forecast:** low. The model beats its baseline by about 3% on held-out data; read the interval, not the midpoint.

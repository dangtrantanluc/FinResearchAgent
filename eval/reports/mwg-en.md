# Research note: MWG (2022–2025)

## Executive summary

- The company implemented a restructuring strategy focused on increasing quality and optimizing operational efficiency to improve growth quality across its chains. [1]

## Revenue

- Net revenue fluctuated from VND 133,405 bn in 2022 to VND 118,280 bn in 2023, before rising to VND 155,928 bn in 2025.
- The 2025 revenue performance exceeded the company's internal plan, with online sales contributing approximately 9,2 trillion VND. [1]
- The Bách Hóa XANH chain reported revenue of nearly 46.900 billion VND in 2025, representing a 14% increase over the previous year. [2]

## Profitability

- Net profit reached VND 7,073 bn in 2025, following a low of VND 168 bn in 2023.
- The net profit growth in 2024 was at 2,124.5% and continued in 2025 at 89.4%.
- Gross margin was 23.1% in 2022 and stood at 19.9% in 2025.
- The Bách Hóa XANH chain achieved its second consecutive year of profitability in 2025, significantly increasing its contribution to the group's total profit. [2]

## Cash flow and capital structure

- Operating cash flow was VND 7,976 bn in 2022 and reached VND 8,517 bn in 2024.
- Free cash flow improved from VND 3,511 bn in 2022 to VND 5,208 bn in 2025.
- Dividends and profits paid were VND 732 bn in 2022 and increased to VND 1,478 bn in 2025.
- The company maintained healthy cash flows to fulfill payment obligations to suppliers and support new shareholder policies, including cash dividends of 1.000 đồng/Cổ phiếu. [1][3]

## Risk signals

- The M-Score of -1.35 is among the highest in the sector (percentile 88); earnings quality deserves a closer look.
- MWG faces risks related to competition and industry saturation, as well as information security and cyber security threats. [4]
- Operational risks identified by the company include inventory management, supply chain disruptions, and potential corruption or waste. [4]

## Forecast

- Based on a 2025 base of VND 155,928 bn, the 2026 revenue is forecast at VND 169,298 bn with a range between VND 123,188 bn and VND 232,666 bn at 80.0% coverage.
- The forecast is primarily driven by inventory growth at +2.0 pp and growth in liabilities at +1.7 pp.
- The company's own plan for 2026 targets a net revenue of 185 nghìn tỷ đồng and a profit after tax of 9.200 tỷ đồng. [5]
- The Bách Hóa XANH chain is expected to drive 2026 performance by contributing approximately 30% of revenue and aiming for revenue growth over 20%. [6]
